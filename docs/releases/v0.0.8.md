# Jellyfesh v0.0.8 "Chorus" — Public Beta

Released **2026-11-27**. The third public beta makes the five apps **one
Jellyfesh with one layout**, Jellyfesh Web's: the same floating sidebar,
pages, Settings and player bar on macOS, Windows, Linux, Android and the
web, each drawn with its own native toolkit.

It also adds:
* **sound quality** in the player bar;
* a **mini player** that stays above other windows;
* **search grouped by source**;
* your sources' **likes and playlists** in the Library;
* **downloads** from your own servers.

It fixes the covers missing from Home, source playlists that wouldn't play
or shuffle, and Android 17's block on home-network servers. The Windows
download is lighter.

A public beta is ready for daily listening but not finished. Please read
[Known limitations](#known-limitations) before relying on it, and report
problems through GitHub Issues.

## Highlights

- **One layout everywhere.** Every app now has Jellyfesh Web's layout:
  * the floating sidebar (Home, Albums, Artists, Songs, Playlists,
    Favourites, Downloads, Search, Sources);
  * phone tabs on narrow screens;
  * Settings as a page in the main window;
  * one player bar floating over every page.

  The apps stay native: no webview, native media keys, menus and
  accessibility.
- **Sound quality in the bar.** A badge in the player bar shows the
  quality of what's playing (Lossless, Hi-Res Lossless, High, Standard).
  * Its menu shows the format ("FLAC · 24-bit · 96 kHz") and lets you
    choose **Auto, Lossless, High, Normal or Low** for the sources that can
    convert.
  * Auto plays lossless, or High on a metered connection.
  * The same choice is Settings › Playback › Streaming in every app.
- **A mini player** with the cover, title, controls and the line being
  sung, above your other windows.
  * Open it from the bar or with Ctrl+M (⌥⌘M on Mac).
  * It is picture-in-picture on Android and in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
- **Your sources' likes and playlists.** Favourites and Playlists now show
  each signed-in source's liked songs, albums and artists, and its
  playlists:
  * Jellyfin, Navidrome and other Subsonic servers, Spotify, NetEase
    Cloud Music, and the community NetEase and QQ Music plugins;
  * live from the source, kept on the device for offline viewing;
  * **Save as Playlist…** makes a Jellyfesh copy.
- **Search by source.** Search has tabs:
  * **All:** your library and every source that allows merged results;
  * **Library**;
  * **one tab per source**, each with Songs, Albums, Artists and
    Playlists and **Show More**.

  Spotify and QQ Music, whose terms forbid mixing, now get tabs of their
  own instead of being left out.
- **Downloads** of songs, albums and playlists from your Jellyfin or
  Navidrome/Subsonic server, when it allows downloading. Files are saved
  in a folder you choose and play offline. You set:
  * the quality: the original file or a smaller conversion;
  * how files and folders are named;
  * whether cover, lyrics and tags are added;
  * whether downloads pause on metered connections.

## What's new

### One Jellyfesh, one layout

The owner asked for every platform to look and work like Jellyfesh Web, and
chose native apps that copy it over the web UI in a webview
([ADR-0023](../adr/0023-one-layout-web-reference.md)).

**The shared layout:**
- **Wide windows:** the floating sidebar panel, with Open Files… and
  Settings at its foot.
- **Up to 760 px wide:** tabs for Home, Library, Search, Sources and
  Settings, with Library switching between its places.
- **Settings is a page in every app.** The macOS Settings window and the
  Linux preferences dialog are gone; ⌘, and Ctrl+, open the page.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts** are listed in Settings › About.
- **The player bar** floats over every page, Settings included, and hides
  only under Now Playing. It now has the device button and More
  everywhere, and the narrow capsule gains Next.

**What stays native:** window frames, the macOS menu bar, file pickers,
media controls and notifications, accessibility, scrolling and the system
font.

The Jellyfesh Web pages are the reference: 68 screenshots of every page,
light and dark, desktop and phone, are in
[`docs/screenshots/web/`](../screenshots/web/).

### Sound quality and the mini player

**Streaming quality** ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-quality-menu-mini-player.md))
is one setting, kept by the core, for every app.
- The quality you choose now reaches the sources. Until now the native
  apps always asked for lossless, and only Jellyfesh Web had a setting.
- Changing it reopens the playing song where it is when the song can
  seek (without counting another play), and applies from the next song
  otherwise.
- With nothing to choose, the menu says why: "Files play as they are." or
  "Quality is set in Spotify.".
- Five plugins had a quality setting of their own, which could play a
  different level from the one the app showed: Jellyfin, Subsonic,
  NetEase, and the community NetEase and QQ Music plugins. Those settings
  are gone, and a level you had set carries over once.

**The mini player** opens from the bar's button or Open Mini Player.
Settings › Appearance › Mini Player offers **Keep on Top**, **Open When
the Window Is Closed** and **Show Lyrics Line**. On each platform it is:
- **macOS:** a floating panel on every Space.
- **Windows:** the compact player, now with the lyrics line.
- **Linux:** above other windows on X11. On Wayland, Keep on Top… opens
  your desktop's window menu.
- **Jellyfesh Web:** a picture-in-picture window.
- **Android:** picture-in-picture when you leave the app, with **Mini
  Player in Picture-in-Picture** turned on.

### Your sources' likes and playlists

Favourites and Playlists now show, after your own hearts and playlists,
one section per signed-in source
([ADR-0024](../adr/0024-source-library-search-by-source.md)).
- **Each section** uses the source's own words: Liked Songs, Favourites,
  Starred.
- **Refreshing:** the sections refresh when the page opens, and the last
  lists show offline with "Updated … ago".
- **A source playlist** opens on its own page with Play, Shuffle and
  **Save as Playlist…**.
- **Spotify playlists** you follow but don't own play by context. Spotify
  lists their songs only in Spotify.
- **Read-only:** the sections never change anything on the service, and
  the heart marks a song in Jellyfesh only.
- **Removed with the source:** a source's kept lists are deleted when you
  sign out of it or uninstall it.

### Search by source

- **Each kind on its own:** search asks every source for songs, albums,
  artists and playlists separately, 20 of each, with Show More for the
  next page.
- **Never mixed:** sources whose terms forbid mixing results come in tabs
  of their own and are never mixed or de-duplicated with others, and the
  All tab links to them.
- **Jellyfesh Web** now uses the same rules: your library is included and
  merging is respected.

### Downloads

Downloads come only from servers that allow them: Jellyfin, and Navidrome
and other Subsonic servers, when your account may download
([ADR-0025](../adr/0025-downloads.md)). Spotify, QQ Music and NetEase
offer no downloads.

**Settings › Downloads:**
- **Folder:** Music/Jellyfesh by default.
- **Quality:** Original keeps the file byte for byte; High, Normal and Low
  are the server's conversion.
- **File Names:** a template with a live preview.
- **Tags:** Add Missing Cover and Lyrics, Lyrics, Save .lrc Files.
- **Pause on Metered Connections.**
- **Downloads at Once.**
- **Remove All Downloads….**

**How downloads behave:**
- **The core runs them:** they resume where they stopped, retry after
  network errors, wait while you're offline or on a metered connection,
  and survive a restart.
- **The Downloads page** shows what's in progress and what's downloaded,
  by album.
- **Offline playback:** a downloaded song plays its file, offline too, and
  carries the Downloaded mark. The download folder joins your library
  folders with the first finished download.
- **Jellyfesh Web** saves a song's file through the browser instead.

### Fixed: covers, playlists and Shuffle

**Covers in Recently Played** (and in Most Played, Favourites and the
restored queue) were missing in the macOS, Windows, Linux and Android
apps. There were four causes:
- a song played from a server kept no picture;
- covers worked only until the app restarted;
- folder art (`cover.jpg`) wasn't used for song rows;
- WebP and GIF covers couldn't be read.

All four are fixed. Songs already in your library pick up their folder's
cover on the next library scan. The macOS app also retries a cover that
failed, instead of giving up on it for the rest of the session.

**Source playlists** that wouldn't play:
- **Spotify:** the core now plays on the active device, the last one you
  chose or played on, or the only one. With several idle devices and none
  chosen, it asks you to pick one in Devices instead of starting music
  somewhere unexpected.
- **NetEase and Spotify:** a playlist is no longer mistaken for an album.
- **Large playlists** start with their first page, without waiting for
  the rest.
- **Shuffle** starts on a random song instead of the first.

### Android 17

Android 17 blocks apps from reaching servers on your home network unless
they ask. Jellyfesh now asks for **Local Network** access before
connecting to a Jellyfin or Navidrome server there. If you said no, it
offers a way to allow it later. Android 8 and 9 ask once for storage
access before the first download.

### Lighter

Measured on this release, not assumed:
- **Playback:** the core already sends nothing while a song plays steadily
  (the apps draw progress and the lyrics bar from one timeline), and
  nothing wakes while paused.
- **Idle Linux app:** wake-ups fell from 2.2 a second to 0.2. A thread
  pool used by the keyring and media controls never retired its last idle
  thread in its newest version, so Jellyfesh holds the version before it.
- **Backdrops:** the cover backdrops Jellyfesh bakes were the one cache
  with no limit. They are now capped at 48 MB, least recently used first.
- **Windows:** the Windows SDK projection, which the app barely uses, is
  no longer precompiled. It is now 24 MiB on each architecture instead
  of about 55, and the installer is 152 MiB, down from 159 MiB in
  v0.0.7. The release build prints these sizes from now on.
- **Jellyfesh Web:** Settings loads only when you first open it, still
  offline too, so the first screen is about 14 KB lighter (gzip). The
  media and plugin parts already loaded only when used.
- **Linux:** the app shows its first window in about a quarter of a
  second.

### Plugins

- **Official plugins 0.0.8:**
  - **Jellyfin** and **Subsonic**: likes, playlists and downloads.
  - **Spotify**: liked songs, albums and playlists. Followed artists need
    a new permission: sign in again to see them.
  - **NetEase Cloud Music**: liked songs and playlists.
- **Community plugins 0.1.3:** NetEase and QQ Music liked songs and
  playlists.
- **Quality levels:** every streaming plugin declares its levels.
- **Five quality settings removed:** the plugins no longer have settings
  of their own for quality.

### Plugin API 1.3

- **New optional exports:**
  - `library`: `likes`, and `playlists` with `owned` and `songs-listed`;
  - `download`: where to fetch a file, for servers that allow it.
- **Manifest:** the `library` and `download` capabilities, and
  `content_policy.qualities`.
- **Compatibility:** plugins for 1.0–1.2 keep loading.
- **Docs:** the plugin guide
  ([`docs/plugins/authoring.md`](../plugins/authoring.md)) explains the
  new exports.

### Core and developer changes

- **The core's contract** for v0.0.8 is in
  [`docs/core-api.md`](../core-api.md) and [`docs/ffi.md`](../ffi.md):
  * the quality settings and `NowPlaying` levels;
  * search groups and `SearchMore`;
  * the `SourceLibrary` and `Downloads` slices;
  * `SetNetworkState`;
  * `address_is_local`, `download_preview` and `audio_extensions`.
- **Library:** `lofty` writes tags, covers and lyrics into downloads.
- **Covers:** `image` now reads WebP and GIF.
- **Plugin manifests:** `library_storage = "none"` is enforced.
- **Security:** `cookie` 0.18.3
  ([RUSTSEC-2026-0594](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0594)).
  Jellyfesh's plugin cookie jars were not affected.

## Signing and trust

- **Official plugins** are signed with the Jellyfesh release key when the
  release is built with `JF_PLUGIN_SIGNING_KEY`.
- **macOS and Windows apps** are signed when their signing secrets are
  set ([`docs/github.md`](../github.md)). Otherwise:
  * macOS asks you to allow the app once in System Settings › Privacy &
    Security;
  * Windows SmartScreen asks you to confirm;
  * Smart App Control on Windows 11 blocks unsigned apps entirely.
- **The Android APK** is only published when the release keystore's
  secrets exist.

## Testing

- **Rust workspace:** every test suite passes, with clippy `-D warnings`,
  `cargo fmt` and `cargo deny` clean. This covers:
  * the core's tests for covers, source playlists, Shuffle, streaming
    levels, search groups, the source library and downloads (a local HTTP
    server with ranges, refusals and resume);
  * the plugin host's API 1.3 sandbox and limit tests;
  * the FFI contract tests.
- **Plugins:** every plugin against a local mock of its service with
  invented data:
  * Jellyfin 49 and Subsonic 55 tests, each with a golden table of what
    every level asks of the server;
  * Spotify 31, NetEase 22, and the community plugins 131.
  * **None was tested against a live service.**
- **macOS:** built and tested by CI.
- **Windows:** built by CI, with 274 portable tests. The start-up test
  launches the published app, opens every page, the phone layout, the
  Downloads page and the mini player, and closes it.
- **Android:** built by CI; 301 logic tests and the FFI checks against the
  real core.
- **Linux:** 210 tests. Every changed screen was checked under Xvfb at
  wide, medium and phone widths against the web's reference screenshots.
- **Jellyfesh Web:** 86 unit tests and 54 end-to-end tests in Chromium,
  against mock Jellyfin and Subsonic servers. They cover hearts,
  playlists, the sources' likes and playlists, Save as Playlist…, the
  quality levels and Download, including a server that refuses it.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet run on devices.** The macOS, Windows and Android apps are
  built and tested by CI, and the Windows app is started there, but the
  project has not run them on a Mac, a PC or a phone. That includes the
  new layout and the mini player.
- **Live services untested.** Every plugin was tested against mock
  services only.
- **Spotify:**
  * playlists you only follow show no song list;
  * followed artists need you to sign in again;
  * Spotify now ends a sign-in after six months.
- **Likes and playlists are read-only.** Jellyfesh doesn't change them on
  the service.
- **Downloads:**
  * They come from Jellyfin and Navidrome/Subsonic only.
  * Subsonic servers other than Navidrome always send the original file,
    whatever quality you choose.
  * Jellyfesh Web downloads one song at a time and has no offline
    library.
- **The mini player:**
  * On Wayland it is a normal window: Keep on Top… opens your desktop's
    window menu, where Always on Top may be offered.
  * In the web it needs Chrome or Edge 116+, or Firefox 151+, on a
    desktop, and it shares the one picture-in-picture window with Desktop
    Lyrics.
- **Per-app gaps:**
  * **Linux:** the sources' lists show their first 100 entries, without
    Show More yet.
  * **macOS:** Settings › Sources and Extensions still use the system's
    grouped forms rather than the web's cards.
- **Carried over from v0.0.7:**
  * Pro is Pro Preview in this beta.
  * The community plugins await the legal review ADR-0012 asks for.
  * Store builds have not been submitted.
  * The legal texts are drafts until a lawyer has reviewed them.

## Upgrading from v0.0.7

Install the new version over the old one; on Windows, run the installer.
Your library, playlists, settings and sign-ins stay, and the bundled
official plugins update to 0.0.8 by themselves. A few things change:
- **A plugin's own quality setting** becomes your Streaming Quality, once.
- **macOS:** Settings opens as a page in the main window.
- **Android 17:** Jellyfesh asks for Local Network access the first time
  it connects to a server at home.
- **Spotify:** sign in again if you want your followed artists.
