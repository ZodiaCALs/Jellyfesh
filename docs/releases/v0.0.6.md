# Jellyfesh v0.0.6 "Luminous" — Public Beta

Released **2026-11-17**. This is Jellyfesh's first public beta. It brings
the **Luminous** design to every app, connects **QQ Music**, **Spotify**
and **NetEase Cloud Music** through new official plugins, prepares **Apple
Music** on macOS, and adds **item links** and **remote playback** to the
core, so that a service's songs can open in its own app or play on your own
devices with Jellyfesh as the remote control.

A public beta is ready for daily listening but not finished. Please read
[Known limitations](#known-limitations) before relying on it, and report
problems through GitHub Issues.

## Highlights

- **Luminous design.** The colour of the cover fills the room: a stronger
  aurora at the artwork's hue on every screen, glass panels that float over
  it, and a Now Playing stage with 380-point artwork, 40-point word-timed
  lyrics and every control in one floating dock.
- **QQ Music** (official API): search, charts, Daily 30, Guess You Like
  and playlists; every song opens in QQ Music.
- **Spotify** (remote control, beta): search and browse your library, then
  play on your own Spotify devices, with a device picker, transfer and
  volume, from every native app.
- **NetEase Cloud Music** (official open platform, beta): your playlists,
  streamed and played by Jellyfesh's own engine.
- **Open in {Service}** wherever a song, album, artist or playlist has a
  link to its service.
- **Plugin API 1.1**, fully compatible with plugins built for 1.0.

## What's new

### The Luminous design

The direction the owner chose from three mock-up directions, now in the
macOS, Windows, Linux and Android apps, Jellyfesh Web and the website
([`docs/ui.md`](../ui.md), "Luminous").

- **Field and glass.** The aurora glows at about 75 % with four larger
  glows at the artwork's hue on every screen. The sidebar is Clear glass,
  grouped surfaces take a Mist tint (without live blur, to keep within the
  glass budget), and the player bar, mini-player and phone tab bar are Veil
  capsules floating above the content, which fades out just above them.
  Each platform uses its own materials: the macOS sidebar material, Mica
  and Acrylic on Windows, `backdrop-filter` on GTK 4.22 and later, blur on
  Android 12 and later; older systems get solid glass.
- **Now Playing as a stage.** The blurred artwork behind everything,
  380-point artwork, a 32-point title, 40-point lyrics that settle in a
  40 ms cascade with inactive lines at 92 %, and one floating dock with
  shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat, the scrubber, volume, the device
  button and More.
- **Type and covers.** 48-point page titles; album covers with larger
  radii and shadows that lift on hover or focus.
- **Accessible.** Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency still give solid
  glass without the cascade. Text keeps WCAG AA contrast on the stronger
  field (checked across the hue circle in light and dark on the web and
  Linux); secondary text moved to a stronger ink where it fell short.
- **The website** opens on a dark aurora with the Jellyfesh lettering as
  glass, a Download button for your system, and the app glowing below it.

The design tokens changed with it (`design/tokens.json`): lyrics 40/1.18,
inactive lyric lines 0.92, the cascade 40 ms, page titles 48, Now Playing
titles 32, grid covers radius 16, and the new `field.aurora-strength`.

### Links and remote playback

- **Open in {Service}.** Sources can declare links that open an item in
  the service itself. Every app offers *Open in {Service}* in the song and
  More menus, on album, artist and playlist pages and in Now Playing's
  Info; an item that cannot play in Jellyfesh opens its link as its main
  action.
- **Remote playback.** A remote source, such as Spotify, plays on your own
  devices. Playing its songs starts a remote session: the player bar,
  mini-player and Now Playing (and the system's media controls) follow the
  service's player, with **Playing on {Device}**, a **Devices** picker to
  move playback, the device's volume, and **Stop Controlling**. The usual
  transport controls and keys work as always. Remote sources' songs never
  join Jellyfesh's queue, and synced lyrics are not shown while they play,
  as their terms require.

### Official plugins

Three new official plugins, bundled with the apps. Each uses the service's
own official interface with **your** account and keys; none works around a
paywall, region lock or other restriction. Set-up guides:
[`docs/plugins/`](../plugins/).

| Plugin | What it does | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **QQ Music** (`org.jellyfesh.qqmusic`) | Search for songs, albums, artists and playlists; browse Daily 30, Guess You Like, AI playlists and charts; open everything in QQ Music. QQ Music's open API returns information and links, not audio, so songs play in QQ Music. | Your personal QQ Music API key, created with your QQ Music account. |
| **Spotify** (`org.jellyfesh.spotify`, beta) | Search and browse your playlists, saved albums and liked songs; play on your Spotify devices with Jellyfesh as the remote. | Spotify Premium, and your own Client ID from a Spotify developer app with `http://127.0.0.1:29817/callback` as its redirect URI. Spotify allows a shared developer app only five users, so each person brings their own. |
| **NetEase Cloud Music** (`org.jellyfesh.netease`, beta) | Your created, saved and starred playlists, streamed at up to lossless where your account allows; VIP and digital-album songs show NetEase's reason instead of playing. | Your own NetEase open-platform App ID and RSA key (an individual developer registration), then sign-in by QR code with the NetEase Cloud Music app. |

QQ Music and Spotify appear in **Add a Source**. NetEase joins the list
once it can search.

### Apple Music on macOS

The macOS app contains an Apple Music integration built on MusicKit's
`ApplicationMusicPlayer`: catalogue search for songs and albums, playback
with Jellyfesh's remote-playback controls, and Apple's isolation rules
(no synced lyrics during Apple Music playback, its own search group,
nothing stored in the library). It is offered only in builds signed with
Jellyfesh's MusicKit identity, and shown only after you agree and
authorisation, a developer-token check and a subscription check all
succeed. **This release's builds are not signed that way, so Apple Music
does not appear in them** (see [Known limitations](#known-limitations)).

### Plugin API 1.1

- The `remote` interface for remote-control sources, in the new
  `remote-source` world, and `[links]` URL templates in the manifest
  ([ADR-0018](../adr/0018-plugin-api-1-1.md)).
- `crypto.rsa-sign-pkcs1-sha256`, which signs with a private key kept in
  the system keystore, so the key never returns to the plugin.
- An optional fixed OAuth redirect port (`[oauth] redirect_port`), for
  services that need an exact redirect URI.
- Plugins built for API 1.0 load unchanged (tested with a plugin built
  against a frozen copy of the 1.0 interface), and plugins built with SDK
  1.1 that declare API 1.0 still install in Jellyfesh v0.0.5.
- `jf-plugin pack` and `verify` check that a plugin declaring `remote`
  exports it.

### Core and developer changes

- `SourceItem.link`, `NowPlaying.link`, `PluginSummary.remote_playback`,
  the new `Remote` slice and event, and `Command::Remote` (refresh,
  transfer, volume, end) in the core, the FFI (UniFFI and the C# records)
  and the JSON contract ([`docs/core-api.md`](../core-api.md)).
- `ExternalPlayer`: apps can register a player of their own (the macOS app
  registers MusicKit's) and the core drives it like a remote source.
- `rsa` 0.10.0, whose constant-time arithmetic fixes RUSTSEC-2023-0071.
- The changelog now follows Keep a Changelog.

## Signing and trust

The official plugins are signed with the Jellyfesh release key (ed25519,
key id `0e446b8fb5b07d74`) only when the release is built with the
repository's `JF_PLUGIN_SIGNING_KEY` secret. Without it, every app shows
them as **Community · unofficial**, off until you allow community plugins,
and **Add a Source** lists no services; [`docs/github.md`](../github.md)
explains how to add the secret and rebuild. The Android APK is signed with
the release keystore when its secrets are set. The macOS and Windows
builds are not code-signed yet (ADR-0016).

  them on Linux, macOS and Windows. The Linux app's 128 tests are among

- **Jellyfesh Web:** 45 Rust tests, 55 unit tests and 30 end-to-end tests

- **macOS:** built and tested by CI (Xcode on macOS 15), with new tests for
  remote playback and the Apple Music logic.
- **Windows:** built by CI, XAML included; 118 portable tests and the C#
  bindings' 11 tests pass here and in CI.
- **Android:** built by CI; 168 logic tests and the FFI checks pass here
  and in CI.
- **Linux:** run under Xvfb with an invented library and a test remote
  source; every changed screen was checked by screenshot.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet run on devices.** The macOS, Windows and Android apps are
  built and tested by CI but have not been run on a Mac, a Windows PC or an
  Android device by the project; the Luminous look there still needs a
  check by eye.
- **Live services untested.** The QQ Music, Spotify and NetEase plugins
  follow each service's documentation as far as it could be read, but have
  only been tested against mock services. Their terms still need the
  owner's reading: QQ Music's key page, Spotify's terms for bring-your-own
  Client IDs and for multi-source players, and NetEase's developer
  agreement.
- **QQ Music** returns no audio: songs open in QQ Music. Albums and
  artists cannot be listed by its open API and open in QQ Music too.
- **Spotify** needs Premium for every playback command and each person's
  own Client ID; port 29817 must be free while you sign in.
- **NetEase Cloud Music** has no search, liked songs, recommendations or
  lyrics yet: their interfaces are not in the documentation available to
  the project. Preview-only songs do not play.
- **Apple Music** is hidden in these builds. It needs an Apple Developer
  Program membership, Jellyfesh's App ID with the MusicKit service, a
  Developer ID provisioning profile and notarization.
- **Remote sessions:** artwork for the remote song shows a placeholder;
  on Android the lock screen and notification keep showing the local track;
  Jellyfesh Web has no remote sessions and refuses remote-control plugins.
- **Glass:** Linux shows solid glass before GTK 4.22; on Windows, shadows
  under rounded covers are rectangular and the fade above the player bar
  is approximated.
- **Jellyfesh Web** loads 569 KiB with gzip on first visit, and keeps no
  folder access between visits (as in v0.0.5).
- Everything in v0.0.5's list that is not mentioned here still applies.

## Upgrading from v0.0.5

Install the new version over the old one; your library, playlists,
settings and sign-ins stay. The bundled official plugins update to 0.0.6
by themselves. Plugins you installed yourself keep working: API 1.0
plugins load unchanged in this version.
