# Jellyfesh v0.0.7 "Verse" — Public Beta

Released **2026-11-22**. The second public beta brings **AI Lyrics**
(synced lyrics made on your device or online with your own key),
**Customize** (the look and layout of Jellyfesh, applied live), and
**Jellyfesh Pro**, which holds them and is free during the beta. It adds a
**Discover** page to every source, **Get Info** with the sound's real
quality, **comments**, a word-by-word **lyrics bar** and **Desktop
Lyrics**. It fixes the Windows app, which did not start at all in v0.0.4
to v0.0.6, and covers that did not show after signing in to an account.
Downloads are simpler too: one file per platform. A
[70-second film](https://zodiacals.github.io/Jellyfesh/#film) on the
website shows what's new, with simulated screens and sample content.

A public beta is ready for daily listening but not finished. Please read
[Known limitations](#known-limitations) before relying on it, and report
problems through GitHub Issues.

## Highlights

- **AI Lyrics** (Pro). For a song with no lyrics, Jellyfesh recognises
  the vocals and times every word. For a song with plain lyrics, it times
  the words you already have. It works **on this device** with an open
  Whisper model you download when you want it, or **online** with your own
  OpenAI, Groq or self-hosted key, and only after you agree to send the
  song.
- **Customize** (Pro). Corners, glass, aurora, typeface, text size,
  density, the lyrics' weight, glow and colour, the order of the sidebar
  and Home, three Now Playing layouts and three mini-player styles, all
  applied as you change them.
- **Lyrics, word by word.** Every app fills the current line word by word
  in Now Playing and in a new lyrics bar. Desktop Lyrics is a floating
  window that can be locked so clicks pass through it. NetEase Cloud
  Music's word-timed lyrics now come through.
- **Discover.** Every source opens on its own Discover page: daily picks,
  charts, new and recent albums, your playlists, whatever the service
  offers.
- **Get Info and comments.** The singer and album pages work for a source's
  songs too, Get Info shows the version, the format and the sound's quality
  ("Hi-Res Lossless", "24-bit · 96 kHz"), and sources with comments get a
  Comments pane.
- **Windows works.** The app now starts, and CI starts it on every change
  to make sure it keeps doing so. It comes as one installer for both kinds
  of PC.

## What's new

### AI Lyrics

The AI Lyrics extension (Settings › Extensions › AI Lyrics) makes
enhanced LRC lyrics with the time of every word
([ADR-0019](../adr/0019-ai-lyrics.md)).

- **Create Synced Lyrics** appears in the Lyrics pane when a song has no
  lyrics and in the song menu; **Time These Lyrics** appears under plain
  lyrics. A job shows its stage (Reading Audio, Sending to {server},
  Recognising… 42 %, Timing Words) and can be cancelled. Made lyrics are
  marked "Made by AI Lyrics · {engine}" and can be made again or deleted.
- **On This Device** (the default) uses whisper.cpp in the app with an
  open Whisper model you download from Settings: Tiny (75 MB), Base
  (142 MB), Small (466 MB) or Large Turbo (547 MB). Downloads are checked
  against published checksums. Nothing leaves your device.
- **Online** sends the song to OpenAI (`whisper-1`), Groq
  (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`) or a server address you give, with your own
  API key from Sign In. Jellyfesh asks before the first song goes to a
  server ("Send This Song to api.openai.com?"), and the plugin host refuses
  to send audio anywhere you have not allowed. Your provider's terms and
  charges apply.
- **Create Automatically** (off by default) makes lyrics for songs that
  start without them.

### Customize

The Customize extension (Settings › Extensions › Customize, or Appearance
› Customize…) changes the look and the layout, live, in every app
([ADR-0020](../adr/0020-customize.md)):

- **Look:** corner roundness, glass, aurora strength, typeface (system,
  rounded, serif, mono), text size and density.
- **Lyrics:** weight, glow and highlight colour (the artwork's, the accent
  or your own).
- **Layout:** which places the sidebar shows and in what order, Home's
  sections and their order, Now Playing as Stage, Artwork or Lyrics, and
  the mini-player as a bar, a compact bar or a floating capsule.

Reset to Defaults brings back the Luminous design of v0.0.6. Plugin
settings now offer sliders, colour wells, ordered checklists and group
headings, and appear in the app's language.

### Jellyfesh Pro

Jellyfesh is free; **Jellyfesh Pro** adds AI Lyrics and Customize
([ADR-0021](../adr/0021-jellyfesh-pro.md)). **During the public beta Pro
is free for everyone (Pro Preview).** Later, Pro will be one purchase in
the App Store, the Microsoft Store and Google Play, or a licence key on
every platform. Settings › Extensions shows Pro's state; Enter Licence
Key… checks keys on the device, with no account and no server. Store
builds use the store's purchase and Restore Purchases.

### Discover, details and comments

([ADR-0022](../adr/0022-discover-details-comments.md))

- **Discover:** choosing a source opens its Discover page, with rows,
  grids and charts and See All; Browse is one button away. Subsonic and
  Navidrome show recently added, most played, recently played and random
  albums; Jellyfin adds continue listening and suggestions; QQ Music shows
  Daily 30, Guess You Like and its charts; Spotify your recent plays, top
  tracks and artists, playlists and saved albums; NetEase your liked songs
  and playlists. Your library's Discover page is Home.
- **The singer and the album:** Go to Artist and Go to Album work for a
  source's songs, with the source's picture and its notes or biography.
- **Get Info** in the song, album and artist menus and in Now Playing's
  Info pane: version, genre, composer, label, and **Audio**: the quality
  (Hi-Res Lossless, Lossless, High, Standard, decided the same way in every
  app), the format being decoded, bitrate, size and ReplayGain.
- **Comments:** a Comments pane in Now Playing for sources that have them
  (the community NetEase Cloud Music plugin), with the popular comments
  first.

### Lyrics bar and Desktop Lyrics

The current line of word-timed lyrics now fills word by word everywhere:
in Now Playing, in a one-line **lyrics bar** in the player bar (above the
mini-player on Android) with its translation, and in **Desktop Lyrics**, a
floating window on macOS, Windows, Linux (kept above other windows on X11)
and Chromium browsers (through Picture-in-Picture). Settings › Appearance ›
Lyrics has Show Lyrics Bar and Desktop Lyrics. All five apps compute the
sweep from the same core helper, checked against the same test vectors.

### Covers, and the same Jellyfesh in every app

- **Covers after signing in.** The macOS, Linux and Android apps never
  fetched a source's covers, and Windows loaded them without the source's
  headers. The core now fetches every source picture through its plugin
  (with the plugin's own headers and allowed hosts) into its cache, and
  every app shows the cached file. Sources that keep covers behind your
  sign-in, such as Subsonic, work too.
- **One app, five platforms.** An audit of every function in every app
  ([`docs/parity.md`](../parity.md)) was followed by fixes everywhere: the
  same sign-in steps (Jellyfin's Quick Connect code, fields with their
  help), full pages for a source's albums, artists and playlists, "Content
  from {Service}", About with the website and licences, the same words and
  empty states, Up Next past 50 songs on Android, and Play and Shuffle
  across the whole library in every app.
- **Resume Playback** now works in every app: the core resumes, and Android
  never starts music by itself after a background start.

### Windows

The Windows app in v0.0.4 to v0.0.6 closed at once, silently, on every PC:
it asked WinUI for its resources before WinUI had loaded them. That is
fixed, together with three more faults found on the way (a keyboard key
name WinUI's XAML reader does not know, a resource-index clash and a loop
in the new start-up guard). The core no longer needs the Visual C++
runtime. Every start-up failure is now logged in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Jellyfesh\logs\app.log` and shown with Open Log Folder
instead of vanishing, and CI starts the published app, opens every page
and closes it on every change. Windows now comes as **one installer** for
x64 and Arm64, per user, without admin rights.

### Jellyfesh Web

- **Signing in to services that refuse browsers** (QQ Music's WeChat
  sign-in, NetEase): Jellyfesh Web now says so before you try, and works
  through **jf-relay**, a small relay you run yourself on your computer or
  your own server ([`docs/web-relay.md`](../web-relay.md)). The project
  runs no relay.
- **Covers** that failed once were missing for the whole visit; they now
  come back, and come through the relay when one is set.
- **A design and motion pass:** pages that kept the previous page's scroll
  position, the player bar's spacing, Settings on phones, staggered lists,
  covers that grow into their album page, press feedback, artwork
  crossfades, loading placeholders, a slowly drifting aurora while music
  plays, and word sweeps that were broken and now animate. Everything
  respects Reduce Motion.
- AI Lyrics online, Customize, Pro, Discover, Get Info, comments and the
  lyrics bar, as in the native apps.

## Signing and trust

The official plugins are signed with the Jellyfesh release key when the
release is built with `JF_PLUGIN_SIGNING_KEY`. The macOS and Windows apps
are signed when their signing secrets are set ([`docs/github.md`](../github.md));
otherwise macOS asks you to allow the app once in System Settings ›
Privacy & Security, and Windows SmartScreen asks you to confirm. Smart App
Control on Windows 11 blocks unsigned apps entirely. The Android APK is
only published when the release keystore's secrets exist.

## Testing

- **Rust workspace:** all tests pass on Linux, macOS and Windows in CI,
  with clippy `-D warnings`, `cargo fmt`, `cargo deny` and the tokens check
  clean. Among them: the core's unit and flow tests (Pro, the look, AI
  Lyrics jobs, Discover, details, covers, resume on launch), the plugin
  host's API 1.2 sandbox and limit tests, `jf-licence`, `jf-relay` (17)
  and `jf-speech` (33).
- **On-device recognition:** CI downloads Whisper Tiny, checks its
  checksums, and recognises synthesised speech with word timing.
- **Plugins:** every plugin against a local mock of its service with
  invented data: AI Lyrics 41 (14 golden alignment cases), Customize 8,
  Subsonic 48, Jellyfin 42, QQ Music 23, Spotify 25, NetEase 21, and the
  community plugins 130 (9 golden word-timing cases). **None was tested
  against a live service.**
- **macOS:** built and tested by CI.
- **Windows:** built by CI, 226 portable tests, and the start-up test that
  launches the published app, opens every page and closes it, from a
  normal folder and from one with Chinese and accented letters.
- **Android:** built by CI; 242 logic tests and the FFI checks against the
  real core.
- **Linux:** 182 tests; changed screens, including a customised look, checked by screenshot under Xvfb.
- **Jellyfesh Web:** 83 Rust tests, 83 unit tests and 43 end-to-end tests
  in Chromium, including signing in through the relay.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet run on devices.** The macOS, Windows and Android apps are
  built and tested by CI, and the Windows app is started there, but the
  project has not yet run them on a Mac, a PC or a phone.
- **Live services untested.** Every plugin was tested against mock
  services only. Some endpoints could not be confirmed (Spotify's
  Development Mode access to top tracks and recent plays; NetEase's open
  platform fields; the unofficial plugins' recommendation, chart and
  comment calls). Spotify users sign in again for the new shelves.
- **AI Lyrics** recognises singing well but not perfectly, above all over
  loud music; made lyrics are labelled and can be made again with a larger
  model. There is no on-device mode in Jellyfesh Web or on Windows on Arm
  (online works there), and on very old x86 processors without AVX2.
  Whether OpenAI and Groq accept requests straight from a browser is
  unconfirmed.
- **Pro** is Pro Preview in this beta. Store products and the licence-key
  shop do not exist yet, and release builds accept licence keys only once
  the owner's public key is set.
- **Customize on Windows** does not yet change corner roundness, text size
  or density. On Linux, corner and text scaling reach Jellyfesh's own
  styles but not libadwaita's built-in widgets (settings rows, switches),
  and Rounded uses the system's sans-serif face. Desktop Lyrics stays above
  other windows only on X11; on Wayland it is a normal window.
- **Jellyfesh Web** has no Create Synced Lyrics in the song menu, no
  Recognising stage (it has no on-device mode), and its album and artist
  pages have no picture or biography from Details yet.
- **QQ Music's** official API gives no song covers, and its songs still
  open in QQ Music.
- **Community plugins** use the services' private web interfaces, may
  break when a service changes, and still await the legal review that
  ADR-0012 asks for.
- **Store builds** have not been submitted; App Review questions about
  plugins and licence keys are listed in [`docs/store-release.md`](../store-release.md).
- **Legal texts** are drafts until a lawyer has reviewed them.

## Upgrading from v0.0.6

Install the new version over the old one (on Windows, run the installer;
your v0.0.6 zip folder can be deleted). Your library, playlists, settings
and sign-ins stay. The bundled official plugins update to 0.0.7 by
themselves, and AI Lyrics and Customize are added. If you used the
unofficial community plugins, they stay installed; new copies are in the
Community Plugins download.
