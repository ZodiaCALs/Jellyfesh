Jellyfesh Privacy Policy

Draft for review by a qualified lawyer before Jellyfesh is sold. This text is not legal advice and is not in force. Words in square brackets, such as [Contact email], are blanks to be filled in.

Last updated: [Effective date]

1. In short

  • Jellyfesh has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The apps send nothing about you, your library or how you use them to us.
  • Your library, playlists, play history, settings and lyrics are kept on your device.
  • Information leaves your device only when you use something that needs it: a source you set up, AI Lyrics in its online mode, a speech model download, the update check of copies downloaded from our website, or a purchase. Each is described below.
  • We hold personal information only if you buy a licence key from us or write to us.

2. Who we are

This policy is made by [Seller legal name] of [Address] ("we", "us"), who makes Jellyfesh. For the information we hold ourselves (section 7), we are the data controller. You can reach us at [Contact email].

It covers the Jellyfesh apps for macOS, Windows, Linux and Android, Jellyfesh Web, the jf command-line player, the official plugins and the Jellyfesh website.

3. What stays on your device

Jellyfesh keeps these on your device, and does not send them to us:

  • Your library: the folders you add, the songs' file locations and tags, artwork, playlists, favourites and play history.
  • Settings: your choices in Settings, the sources and plugins you have installed and their settings (such as a server address), the providers you have allowed AI Lyrics to send audio to, and your licence key if you enter one.
  • Sign-ins: tokens, cookies and keys that let a plugin stay signed in to a service, and the API key you give AI Lyrics. Jellyfesh keeps these in your system's keychain: the Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, the Secret Service on Linux and storage encrypted with the Android Keystore on Android. A plugin's cookies are kept in a file that is encrypted with a key held in the keychain. Jellyfesh never stores your passwords: where a service signs in with a password, it is exchanged once for a token.
  • Lyrics made by AI Lyrics and the speech models you download.

Uninstalling a source in Jellyfesh deletes its settings and sign-in, and removing a folder takes its songs out of the library (the files stay where they are). Removing the Jellyfesh app itself can leave its data folder and keychain entries on your device, which you can delete. Backups you make of your device, and Android's own backup if it is turned on, can include Jellyfesh's data; they are handled by you or your backup provider.

4. What leaves your device, and when

Sources you set up. Online sources work through plugins. When you use a source, its plugin contacts the server or service you set up, directly from your device: for example your own Jellyfin, Navidrome or other Subsonic server, or QQ Music, Spotify or NetEase Cloud Music with your own account, key or credentials. The plugin sends what the service needs to do what you asked: your sign-in, what you search for, browse or play, and, for Spotify, the commands for the device you control. Each plugin can only contact the servers its package names. That service's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. We do not receive any of it.

Community plugins are made by others or are unofficial. They contact the services they name in the same way, within the same limits, but we do not control what they send. They stay off until you allow community plugins.

AI Lyrics, online mode. Only if you choose the online mode, and only after you allow it for a provider, AI Lyrics sends the audio of the song you are making lyrics for (as 16 kHz mono sound, in parts of up to eight minutes) to the speech recognition provider you chose, such as OpenAI, Groq or a server you name, with your own API key. The request can also include hints that help recognition, such as the song's language or the lyrics you are timing. The provider's terms and privacy policy apply, and the provider may charge your account. You can stop allowing a provider at any time in Settings, under Extensions, AI Lyrics, Sending Audio.

AI Lyrics, on this device. In this mode, which is the default, the song's audio does not leave your device. The speech models it uses are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) only when you choose to download one; Hugging Face receives the request, including your IP address, as for any download.

Update check. Copies of Jellyfesh downloaded from our website or our releases page ask GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists, at most once a day while Check for Updates Automatically is on, and when you choose Check for Updates. The request carries nothing that identifies you beyond what any internet request carries, such as your IP address. Copies from an app store and Jellyfesh Web do not do this.

Links you open. Open in a service, and links in Jellyfesh, open the service's app or your browser, which then work under their own terms.

5. Purchases and licence keys

In an app store. If you buy Jellyfesh Pro in Apple's App Store, the Microsoft Store or Google Play, the store handles the purchase and your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Jellyfesh asks the store, on your device, whether you own Jellyfesh Pro. We do not receive your payment details. The stores give us reports about sales, which do not identify you by name.

Licence keys. If you buy a licence key from [Licence key shop], the shop processes your payment. We receive [the order details: your name, email address, country, the product, the price and the order number] to deliver your key, to help you if you lose it, and to keep the records that tax law requires.

A licence key contains a random licence number, the product, the day it was issued and a signature. It does not contain your name or email address. Jellyfesh checks licence keys on your device; it does not contact us or anyone else to activate or check a key.

6. What we do not do

We do not run analytics, crash reporting or advertising in Jellyfesh, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal information. App stores and operating systems may collect information under their own policies, such as crash reports and usage statistics you agreed to share with developers, and give us summaries of it; we use those only to improve Jellyfesh.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to [Contact email]. You may also complain to a data protection authority, such as [Supervisory authority].

9. Jellyfesh Web

Jellyfesh Web runs in your browser. The website it comes from sends the app's files and receives nothing back from the app. Jellyfesh Web talks only to the servers you sign in to. It keeps your settings, library and plugins in your browser's storage, with sign-ins encrypted using a key the browser keeps; it uses no cookies. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it. If you host Jellyfesh Web yourself, your own server's practices apply to it.

10. Our website

The Jellyfesh website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It remembers the language you choose in your browser's local storage. GitHub, which hosts the site, may log requests, including IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

11. Security

Connections to online services use HTTPS. A server you add yourself is reached at the address you give: if that address starts with http://, the connection is not encrypted. Plugins run in a sandbox that limits what they can do and which servers they can contact. Sign-ins are kept in your system's keychain. No system is perfectly secure; keep your devices up to date.
