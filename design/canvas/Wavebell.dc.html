3 · Wavebell

The bell drawn as sound: two wave arcs over a solid core, with the tentacles as a level meter. Built to stay legible at 16 px.

Warm coral to amber (hues 32° to 78°), after the sea nettle, with a white glyph. The most legible and most distinctive in a dock full of blue icons. It leans towards a broadcast or fingerprint sign, so check it against podcast and radio app icons.
