Icon B in ten colour combinations

Cyan to deep blue, ice-to-cyan lines. The approved construction.

3 · Royal

Sky cyan into royal blue; the bluest of the set.

4 · Teal Deep

Teal into deep sea blue; greener and calmer.

5 · Sea Glass

Pale aqua into teal-blue; the softest and most Misty-like.

6 · Electric

Vivid cyan into electric blue; the most saturated, loud in a dock.

7 · Steel

Blue-grey with cyan lines; quiet and professional.

8 · Abyss (dark)

Navy to near-black with a cyan glow; works as the dark-mode icon.

9 · Ice (light)

Ice white to pale cyan with deep blue lines; a light appearance.

10 · Lagoon

Bright lagoon into azure, white lines and a deeper shadow.
