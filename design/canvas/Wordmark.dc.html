Wordmark options

Options 1 to 3 are drawn in the icon's own line style. Options 4 to 6 are set in Inter Display, an openly licensed typeface, and converted to outlines. Each one is shown on light and on dark.

1 · Monoline

Lettering drawn with the icon's round strokes. The j, y and l's curl like tentacles. No font involved.

2 · Monoline with bell

The j's dot becomes a small dome: the jellyfish's bell.

3 · On the icon's tile

The monoline wordmark in the icon's translucent lines on its own gradient, for the website header, splash and social cards.

4 · Type

Inter Display SemiBold, kerned and outlined, with the icon's gradient. The most neutral.

5 · Type with bell

Lowercase, with the j's dot swapped for the dome.

6 · Spaced capitals

Inter Display Medium, widely spaced. Quiet and premium, closer to hi-fi equipment branding.
