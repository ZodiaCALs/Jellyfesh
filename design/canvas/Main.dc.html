Five directions for one jellyfish

All five are shown as macOS tiles at the same size. Use the Ground tweak to see them on a dark dock.

1 · Lumen

Frosted-glass bell lit by the aurora; tentacles fall like a soft waveform.

2 · Abyss

The same bell after dark: a luminous outline drifting in a deep field.

3 · Wavebell

The bell drawn as sound waves, the tentacles as a level meter. Built for 16 px.

4 · Verse

Swimming bell-first, its tentacles trail behind as lines of lyrics.

5 · Tide

Misty's identity-glyph recipe: one outline glyph on a hue-tinted tile.
