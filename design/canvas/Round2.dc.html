Wavebell's shape in Tide's style

Eight variants in deep blue and cyan, with three kinds of transparent line: translucent, glass, and an outlined core. Use the Ground tweak to see them on a dark background.

1 · Tide Classic

Tide's tile recoloured cyan to deep blue, with solid white lines. The baseline to compare against.

2 · Clearwater

Same tile; the lines are 75% white, so the gradient shows through them.

3 · Glass Tide

Deep blue to cyan, reversed. Each line is a frosted tube: a bright rim and a clear core.

4 · Deep Sea

Navy lit by a cyan glow from above. The glass lines shade from white to cyan.

5 · Depth

A cyan surface falling to deep blue. Translucent lines, with an outlined core like Tide's bell.

6 · Midnight

Near-black navy with a cyan rim of light, and cyan-tinted translucent lines.

7 · Aurora

Deep blue with cyan and azure glows in the corners, like Misty's field, and glass lines.

8 · Lagoon

The brightest: cyan to azure, with 88% white lines and a deeper shadow for lift.
