## Music

Original, made for this video by `music/make_music.py`: synthesised from
sine waves and noise with numpy, with no samples, loops or recordings of any
kind. Its composition and the code are part of Jellyfesh.

## Sample content

Every artist, album, song title, lyric line, comment, account name and
source name in the video is invented (`scene/content.js`). The album covers
are abstract gradients generated by `tools/make_assets.py`. Any likeness to
real releases or people is unintended.

The Jellyfesh icon and lettering come from `website/assets` (Copyright ©
