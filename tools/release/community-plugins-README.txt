Jellyfesh community plugins {version}
=====================================

This zip holds the two unofficial community plugins for Jellyfesh:

  community.unofficial.netease.jfpkg   NetEase Cloud Music (Unofficial)
  community.unofficial.qqmusic.jfpkg   QQ Music (Unofficial)

With them you sign in to your own NetEase Cloud Music or QQ Music account by
QR code, then search, play your liked songs and playlists, and see synced
lyrics. They are not signed by Jellyfesh: they use the services' private web
interfaces, may stop working whenever a service changes, and may be against
the service's terms. Use them only with your own account, for personal use.
No plugin gets around a paywall: songs a service keeps for its subscribers
stay unavailable.

The Jellyfesh apps on the release page and in app stores do not include
these plugins. Builds of Jellyfesh made from its source code already do.


Installing them
---------------

In Jellyfesh for macOS, Windows, Linux or Android. (Jellyfesh Web can use
them only through a relay you run yourself, because these services do not
let web pages connect to them directly; see the Jellyfesh documentation.)

1. Unzip this file.
2. Open Settings, then Sources, and turn on Allow Community Plugins.
   Jellyfesh first explains what community plugins are; read it and agree.
3. Choose Install from File... next to Installed, and pick one of the
   .jfpkg files. Do the same for the other one if you want both.
4. Turn the plugin on, accept its notice, and sign in with the QR code.

To remove one, choose Uninstall... under it in Settings, Sources.
