## 7. AI Lyrics

AI Lyrics (a Pro feature) makes synced lyrics for a song by recognising
its vocals with a speech recognition model. It works in one of two ways
that you choose in its settings:

* **On This Device:** the recognition runs on your device. The speech
  model is downloaded only when you ask for it.
* **Online:** Jellyfesh sends the song's audio to the speech recognition
  provider you choose (for example OpenAI, Groq or a server you name),
  using your own API key, and only after you have allowed sending audio to
  that provider. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what
  you send, and the provider may charge your account. You are responsible
  for your account with the provider and for any charges.

Lyrics made by AI Lyrics are machine-made. They may mishear, leave out or
add words, and may be timed wrongly. Jellyfesh labels them as made by AI.
You are responsible for having the right to process the audio and the
lyrics you use with AI Lyrics, and for how you use the lyrics it makes.
Made lyrics are kept on your device for your own listening; do not publish
or share lyrics that you have no right to publish or share.
